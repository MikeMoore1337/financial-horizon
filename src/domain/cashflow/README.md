# Cashflow projection v1 - Issue #36

Pure, deterministic TypeScript calculation (`forecastCashflow`) plus a neutral account/reserve snapshot adapter (`adaptCashflowSources`). **Not wired** to `finance.js`, IndexedDB, React, #33/#6/#39, the financial API, or PostgreSQL. Source-only: no migration, network request, state mutation, browser storage, or banking side effects.

## DTO contract and financial rules

- All amounts are `Number.isSafeInteger` **kopecks**; reject fractions, negative magnitudes, invalid dates, duplicate IDs and overflow. Currency is RUB; the source adapter requires a nonempty explicitly confirmed account list with `currency: "RUB"` and rejects foreign currencies. Exchange rates are not modeled. `openingBalanceCents` is a confirmed, **consolidated** bank/cash balance at the **start** of `openingDate`. It may be negative. `openingReservedCents` is already within that money, is nonnegative, and is **not a second account**. `expectedAvailableCents = expectedBalanceCents - expectedReservedCents`; a negative result is a potential shortage, not an authorized overdraft.
- `asOfDate` is supplied by the caller. The engine has no `Date.now()`, locale-sensitive parsing, timezone or implicit "today". `endDate = addMonthsAnchored(asOfDate, 1|3|6|12)`, inclusive; all dates use strictly validated `YYYY-MM-DD` Gregorian calendar arithmetic and UTC only as a pure mathematical calendar representation. Recurrence `everyMonths` (1..60) uses the **original** day-of-month, clamped for 29/30/31, with no February drift. `untilDate` is inclusive against the unmodified anchor occurrence.
- `plans` contain unique planned external inflows or obligations/irregular/loan outflows. The supplied `amountCents` is the **whole** occurrence, not the residual. The date and total for an individual occurrence can be replaced by `overrides` keyed by `{planId, occurrenceDate}`; cancellation removes only that due occurrence. Cancelling the entire plan removes all occurrences. Adjusting the plan or its overrides and recalculating produces a new result without persistent side effects.
- `actuals` contain confirmed cash movements through `asOfDate`, at most one **canonical logical movement per ID**. They affect the confirmed bank balance from `openingDate` inclusive. Facts predating the opening snapshot may be used to settle a future plan but do not change that opening bank balance again. A linked payment must specify both `planId` and the **original** `occurrenceDate`; installments may be partially paid via multiple distinct facts; total linked non-voided amount cannot exceed the occurrence's effective amount (even for dates beyond the selected horizon). A void is supplied by `voided: true`; an append-only ledger adapter must normalize its original transaction and reversal **once**, not pass both the logical void and its compensating posting. An unmatched actual changes confirmed bank cash exactly once but does not automatically discharge an unrelated plan. This prevents guessing linkage.
- An internal own-account `transfer` is ignored in **consolidated external cashflow**, including plan-vs-fact and available cash. Account-specific future balances, foreign-currency conversion, transfer fees and funds held outside the supplied accounts require separate explicit DTOs; do not disguise them as consolidated income/expense.
- **Past due unpaid expense** since the opening snapshot is forecast as a catch-up payment **on `asOfDate`**. This reduces *hypothetical* expected balance/availability, not the separately reported `confirmedAsOfBalanceCents`. Full or partial booked settlement reduces it exactly once. Outstanding obligations with original effective dates **before** `openingDate` cannot safely be inferred from an opening bank balance: callers must explicitly carry those debts forward as a new plan occurrence/adjusted due date. **Past missed income is not assumed to arrive later**; `totalUnrealizedIncomeCents` reports the missing amount instead. Future income is flagged as `estimated` unless explicitly marked `confirmed`; even “confirmed” here is a caller-provided claim, **not** a bank guarantee.
- `reserves` are earmarking movements only: `allocate` increases locked money, `release` reduces it. Neither changes bank cash. Actual reserve movements through `asOfDate` and planned movements on/after `asOfDate` are applied; missed historical plans to reserve money are **not** auto-charged. Negative reserved totals are rejected. **Release when a payment spends reserved funds must be explicitly projected/confirmed** by the #6 adapter; a payment debits bank cash once, and the release removes only the lock. If a release is omitted, available cash stays conservatively lower. Reserve balances are not free income and cannot be silently used to cover an unfunded payment.
- Early loan repayment is a confirmed expense **only when it actually occurs**; revised future installments and cancellation of stale ones must come from the loan schedule/ledger adapters. Interest/principal splits are *not* separate expenses. The engine intentionally does not guess credit payoff dates or bank contractual schedules.
- Plan-vs-fact compares daily *planned* and *confirmed* external movements between opening date and `asOfDate`, and reports their net variance. Future projected entries use remaining (unpaid, uncancelled) amounts. `confirmedBalanceCents` is populated only up to `asOfDate`. `expectedBalanceCents` and `expectedAvailableCents` after that are **scenarios**, not actual bank values; at `asOfDate` the expected amount additionally includes the modeled catch-up of overdue outflows. `occurrences` preserves original anchors, effective dates, type and paid/remaining amounts for a future calendar adapter. `minimumExpectedAvailableCents`, `minimumExpectedBalanceCents` and `firstCashGap` concern dates from `asOfDate` through `endDate`, not older historical lows.
- All input plans, factual postings and reserve movements must come from **one consistent authorized profile and the same opening snapshot**. This module does not authenticate, de-duplicate SQL transactions, execute account transfers, or provide atomicity; #33/#67 must supply a complete, authorized, revised snapshot and idempotent linkage. Multi-user and offline/backup storage are untouched. A history window >2,000 days, >20,000 occurrences or unsafe arithmetic is rejected, not approximated.
